An alternative number for the US is 1-800-976-5797, which is also toll-free and available seven days a week from 9:00 AM to 11:00 PM Eastern Time [citation:4]. This number may be useful if you experience issues with the primary number or if you prefer to call during slightly different hours.

When to Call Ancestry Customer Service
======================================

Knowing when to call Ancestry customer service versus using online resources helps you get the most efficient assistance.

Call Ancestry customer service if you are experiencing billing issues, such as unexpected charges or problems with subscription renewals. If you have questions about your membership plan or need help upgrading or canceling your subscription, speaking with a representative can clarify your options.

If you have issues with your AncestryDNA kit, such as lost kits, damaged kits, or delayed results, calling customer service is recommended. Representatives can assist with kit registration, provide status updates, and arrange replacements if needed.

If you are locked out of your account or experiencing login issues that password reset cannot resolve, contacting customer service by phone can expedite account recovery. If you have questions about your family tree, records, or DNA results that require personalized guidance, speaking with a representative can be helpful.

Preparing for Your Call
=======================

Preparing before you call can make your interaction with Ancestry customer service more efficient and productive. Having the right information ready will help the representative assist you faster.

Before you call, gather your account information, including your username or email address associated with your Ancestry account. If you are calling about a specific issue such as a billing discrepancy or a DNA kit, have any relevant details handy. If you are calling about an AncestryDNA kit, be prepared to provide the kit registration code or the order number.

If you have previously contacted Ancestry support about the same issue, have your case or ticket number available. Check the support hours for your region before calling to ensure that a representative is available. If you are unable to call during business hours, you can also contact Ancestry by email through their support website.
